# EOSAT-1 thermal network — design note

**Date:** 2026-11-03. **Branch:** `thermal-model`.
**Asked for by:** Franz, after `OBC_THERMAL_ANALYSIS.md`: "Move [OBC limits] towards hardware, plus model more
realistic cooling in the simulator (less power used = colder spacecraft). If possible, build a proper thermal
simulation." and "Yes, make it physical".
**Code:** `sim/packages/smo-simulator/src/smo_simulator/models/thermal_network.py` (solver, view factors, fluxes),
wired into `tcs_basic.py`. **Configuration:** `sim/configs/eosat1/subsystems/tcs.yaml` `network:` and
`simulation.yaml` `tcs.environment`. **Tests:** `sim/tests/test_simulator/test_thermal_network.py`.
**Results:** `OBC_THERMAL_ANALYSIS.md`, section "After the thermal network".

## 1. What changed

The zone model had each heated zone relax towards a configured sink (+10 °C in eclipse, +12 °C in sun for
the OBC; −8/0 °C for the battery; an external sink with a β gain for the panels). Nothing that was powered
or switched off changed those sinks, so safe mode, load shed and payload state had no thermal effect.

It is replaced by a lumped-parameter network of 16 nodes:

```
C_i dT_i/dt =  Σ_surfaces [ α S A max(0, n·ŝ) f_sun          direct sun (attitude, penumbra)
                          + α S a A F(n) max(0, r̂·ŝ)         Earth albedo
                          + ε q_IR A F(n)                     Earth IR
                          − ε A σ T_i⁴ ]                      radiation to space
             + Σ_j G_ij (T_j − T_i)                           conduction
             + Σ_j (εA)_ij σ (T_j⁴ − T_i⁴)                    internal radiation
             + P_i                                            electrical dissipation + heaters
```

* `ŝ`, `n̂_nadir` in the body frame come from the AOCS model's **true** quaternion and the propagator
  (same rule EPS uses for array power: AOCS ticking → true attitude; otherwise a tumbling body with the
  orientation averages ¼ for the sun and (1 − cos ρ)/2 for the Earth).
* `F(n)` is the closed-form flat-plate Earth view factor (full view, partial view, no view), checked for
  continuity and against the sphere average in the tests.
* `S` = 1361 W/m² × (1 AU / r)², `r` from the propagator's Sun vector (1322 W/m² in July, 1414 in January).
* `P_i` is what the EPS **actually bills** on each line, I × V as `eps_basic` computes it, mapped to nodes
  (table §3). Switching a load off cools the node that carried it. Heaters are applied from TCS's own heater
  state (so an open-circuit heater bills but does not heat); the unattributed rest of the bus load (noise, an
  injected bus short) goes to the structure; the battery gets I²R plus the charge inefficiency.
* Electrical power generated by a face's cells is subtracted from that face's absorbed sun (it leaves as
  electricity and reappears as load dissipation).
* The **FPA** keeps its controlled first-order law and cooler model (`_fpa_achievable_lift_k`, unchanged);
  its sink is now the **payload** node instead of the fixed +12 °C, and the heat it gains or loses is taken
  from that node, so energy is conserved. The cooler's 15 W of input is dissipation on the structure.
* **Integrator:** explicit Euler, internally sub-stepped to ≤ 0.4 × the smallest node time constant at the
  current temperatures (radiation linearised as 4εσAT³) and ≤ 20 s. Stable for any caller step; at
  `speed.max` = 1000× the engine already hands the models 1.67 s sub-steps. Cost ≈ 0.1 ms per TCS tick.
* **Noise** moved from the physics to the measurement: published temperatures carry σ = 0.05 K thermistor
  noise (`sensor_noise_k`); the node temperatures themselves are deterministic.

Unchanged: every parameter ID, HK structure, telecommand (`HEATER_*`, setpoints, auto mode, duty limits,
FPA cooler / setpoint, decontamination), thermostat logic, failure names and the instructor API.

## 3. Surfaces, couplings, dissipation

**External surfaces** (6U envelope 100 × 226 × 366 mm; cell fractions = `eps.yaml` cell area / face area):

| Surface | Area (m²) | Coating | α | ε |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ±X | 0.0827 | 73 % TJ GaAs cells + 27 % Z-93 white | 0.71 | 0.87 |
| ±Y | 0.0366 | 82 % cells + 18 % white | 0.78 | 0.86 |
| +Z | 0.0226 | 80 % cells + 20 % white | 0.76 | 0.86 |
| −Z | 0.0226 | 85 % Z-93 radiator + 15 % black aperture/baffle | 0.28 | 0.91 |
| wings, front | 0.143 each | 84 % cells + 16 % PCB | 0.88 | 0.85 |
| wings, back | 0.143 each | bare PCB | 0.75 | 0.85 |

(α of the cell faces is before the electrical extraction; with the array loaded the effective α of the cells
drops by about 0.2.)

**Conductors** (W/K): panels–structure ±X 1.5, ±Y 1.0, +Z 0.6, **−Z 0.04**; wings–±Y panel 0.05 (hinges;
1.0 while stowed); obc–structure 0.6; battery–+Y panel 0.25 and –structure 0.05; ttc–structure 0.8;
payload–−Z panel 1.5, payload–structure 0.02; aocs–structure 1.0; prop–structure 0.4, prop–−X panel 0.3;
thruster–prop 0.0417. **Internal radiation** ((εA)_eff, m²): ±X panels 0.03, ±Y 0.015, +Z 0.01, −Z 0.003,
obc 0.01 — all to the structure.

**Dissipation map** (share of each EPS line's billed power): `obc` 6 W → 50 % obc node (OBC 3 W), 50 %
structure (PDU/EPS housekeeping 3 W); `ttc_rx` → ttc; `ttc_tx` → 85 % ttc (≈15 % leaves as RF: 0.5 W of 5 W,
2 W of 11 W); `payload` → payload; `fpa_cooler` → structure (rejection strap to the frame); `aocs_wheels`
→ aocs; `prop` → prop (the catbed heater's 12 W → thruster).

Design choices worth knowing:

* **Battery on the +Y wall.** EOSAT-1's β is −21° to −31° all year, so the Sun is always on the −Y side and
  +Y is the cold, never-sunlit face (the star tracker ST2 is canted to +Y for the same reason). The battery
  therefore runs colder than the electronics, which keeps it in its charge range without cooking it.
* **Imager thermal island.** The 15 W cooler runs whenever the payload is in STANDBY or IMAGING; with it on,
  the whole bus sits about 15 K warmer. If the imager were bolted to the frame its sink would follow, and the
  cooler (whose lift falls with sink temperature) could not hold −15 °C. The imager and its −Z radiator
  are therefore isolated from the frame (0.04 + 0.02 W/K, low-e back), the arrangement real cooled imagers
  use. The FPA keeps its ~2–5 K of regulation margin in nominal operations and loses it, as it should, in
  the hot case.

## 5. Calibration

The free parameters were the conductances and the coating of the non-cell area; everything else is a
datasheet value or follows from the geometry. They were tuned with a standalone harness (idealised nadir
geometry, β −30°) and then checked in the full engine (`OBC_THERMAL_ANALYSIS.md`, "After the thermal network")
against these targets:

| Target (design intent) | Result (engine, `OBC_THERMAL_ANALYSIS.md` §8) |
|---|---|
| Nominal: battery +5…+25 °C with its heater under thermostat | +16…+22 °C with the cooler running (heater idle); +5…+10 °C under thermostat with the cooler off (7 % duty) |
| Nominal: OBC +10…+35 °C | +22…+33 °C (cooler running), +8…+20 °C (cooler off) |
| Long safe mode / load shed in an eclipse-heavy season drives the OBC towards ≈ −5 °C average with lows of −10…−20 °C, so its heater matters | load shed −2.9 °C (lows −9.1); cold season −6.7 °C (lows −12.6), below the −10 °C heater ON point at each eclipse; safe mode +5.0 °C |
| FPA: −15 °C holdable in nominal operations, margin falling in the hot case | held, ≈ 2 K of margin in STANDBY; lost (+5 °C) when imaging all orbit in the hot case |
| Cold soak: each zone's default depth recoverable by its heater | yes for all three (§6) |
| Energy conservation | stored-energy change = ∫(in − out) dt within 1 J per 3 h window |

## 6. Limits and the OBC heater

* OBC limits moved towards hardware: **yellow −10 °C, red −20 °C** (iOBC-class −25 °C rating with 5 K
  margin); high side unchanged (yellow +60, red +70 — already within the 65–85 °C ratings). Changed in
  `mcs/limits.yaml`, S12 0x0406 (low limit 0 → −20 °C), TCS events (warning −10, alarm −20).
* OBC heater thermostat **ON −10 °C / OFF −5 °C** (was +5 / +10, which made every heater cycle a yellow
  alarm). 4 W across 0.6 W/K = 6.7 K of authority. It is a survival heater: idle in nominal operations,
  working in long load shed / cold-season survival and against a cold soak.
* Battery limits unchanged (yellow +2, red 0 °C: Li-ion is not charged below 0 °C).

`cold_soak` is now an extra heat leak `G_zone × offset` out of the zone (as if its mounting interface were
`offset` colder) plus the immediate step, with `G_zone` the zone's own mount conductance. A blank offset
takes the zone default (`tcs.yaml network.cold_soak.default_offset_c`):

| Zone | G_zone (W/K) | Heater authority P/G | Default | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| battery | 0.30 | 8 W → 27 K | −25 K | measured −26 °C passive (red); the heater brings it back to +3…+10 °C |
| obc | 0.6 | 4 W → 6.7 K | −20 K | the leak also drains the frame through the mount, so the zone moves ~1.4× the offset: from the cooler-off nominal (+15 °C) it lands at −13.6 °C mean (yellow, below the −10 °C ON point) and the heater brings it back to −8.6 °C, cycling. −40 K went to −42 / −34 °C (red either way) |
| thruster | 0.0417 | 12 W → 288 K | −25 K | unchanged: the catbed heater wins anything |

## 7. Limitations

* Lumped nodes: no gradients inside a node (a panel is one temperature; the stack is one board).
* No MLI is modelled — EOSAT-1 as configured has none; an effective-emittance blanket (ε* 0.01–0.05) can be
  added as a surface on any node.
* Wings do not shadow the body and the body does not shadow the wings; no inter-face external radiation.
* Albedo uses the `max(0, r̂·ŝ)` sub-satellite approximation; short-term albedo/OLR extremes over
  particular regions are not modelled (orbit-average constants).
* TCS (and therefore the network) is not ticked in spacecraft phases 0–1 (bootloader, engine rule), so the
  spacecraft does not cool between separation and application boot.
* The FPA is still a controlled first-order node, not a physical cold finger.
* EPS still uses its fixed `array_temp_sunlit_c` (65 °C) for the cell temperature loss rather than the panel
  node temperatures.
