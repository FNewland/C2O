## Explainer candidates

1. **"A telecommand's life: from Send to S1.7."** Sources: `sim/docs/PUS_SERVICE_REFERENCE.md` (S1); ch17 §17.3 CCSDS protocol stack. Outline: what the packet utilisation standard (PUS) is, then service 1, request verification, report by report. Accepted (S1.1) is not the same as done (S1.7). Failures come at acceptance (S1.2) or at execution (S1.8), and the notice text matters more than the code. Long-running commands (the boot CRC window) show the gap. Animation: Manim timeline with packets moving up and reports coming back, branching on failure. Prepares WB-5.5-L001 q5, the V-OPS-04 refusal question, and card reading for every later card. Audience: whole team.
2. **"The rate ladder and its gates."** Sources: `LINK_BUDGET_AND_RATES.md` §1, §2, §6.3, §9.5; ch11 §11.6. Outline: why a spacecraft has several downlink rates (the margin vs rate trade-off), SAFE / LOW / HIGH, the on-board gates (beacon mode, antenna, DOWNLINK mode) vs the ground policy (the 10° rule), why all the refusals report 0x0007, and the "three things called beacon". Animation: Motion Canvas state machine plus a Manim margin-vs-rate plot from `link_budget_ladder.py`. Prepares LEOP-001 Step 5a, WB-5.5-L001/L002, and later NOM-011 dumps. Audience: ttc in depth; whole team for the gate idea.
3. **"Housekeeping: reports, SIDs, enabled vs requested."** Sources: PUS_SERVICE_REFERENCE S3 (subtypes 5, 6, 27); OPERATIONS_GUIDE § Enable Periodic Housekeeping; ch12 §12.3.4. Outline: what housekeeping is, structure identifiers, periodic vs one-shot (HK_ENABLE vs HK_REQUEST), the boot default [1, 11], why powering a unit never enables its report, and why "dark" is checked through line flags. Animation: Motion Canvas SID grid lighting up through the LEOP sequence, driven by the fly-through test's command log. Prepares NOM-001 Step 5, LEOP-001 Step 5, LEOP-002, and HK-missing off-nominal lines. Audience: whole team (fdir_systems commands it).
4. **"From alarm to decision."** Sources: ch18 §18.5; ch19 §19.5.9–19.5.10; `s12_definitions.yaml`. Outline: an S12 limit rule, alarm states, ACK = ownership, the loop call, the anomaly ticket fields, hypothesis discipline, and the FD call. Animation: Motion Canvas. Prepares WB-5.5-AT. Audience: whole team. (This partly overlaps V-OPS-03. Make it only if V-OPS-03 cannot carry the S12 rule itself.)
