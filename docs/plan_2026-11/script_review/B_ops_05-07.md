## Explainer candidates

### 1. The orbit energy balance: why a margin is an average, not a reading (3–5 min)

- **Textbook:** `08_architecture_interfaces_budgets.md` §8.4.4 "Orbit-average power";
  `09_power_and_thermal.md` §9.2 "Solar array sizing", §9.3.2 "Sizing equations";
  `22_derivations.md` §V.11 "Solar Array Sizing", §V.5 "Eclipse Fraction";
  `07_orbit_and_environment.md` §7.1.2.
- **Outline:** Energy in during the sunlit arc, energy out all orbit, and the battery
  as the buffer. An instant reading in sunlight (charging) can hide a negative
  orbit-average margin, because the eclipse is part of the orbit too. The video then
  shows how the cos θ incidence factor turns stowed, tumbling, deployed and sun-pointed
  into four very different averages, ending on the console's "This orbit in / out, Wh"
  readout and the orbit-average margin row.
- **Route:** `powerflow.js` ui-viz replay of four fly-through segments, plus a Motion
  Canvas "energy bucket" and a Manim cos θ plot.
- **Prepares:** the V-OPS-05 questions on the negative margin and the "20 W all orbit
  long" true/false; LEOP-004 Step 7 and LEOP-005 Step 5 (power-positive GO); LEOP-007
  Step 6 ("margin positive").
- **Audience:** whole team; `eps_tcs` must have watched it.

### 2. B-dot and magnetic control: what a magnetorquer can and cannot do (4–6 min)

- **Textbook:** `10_aocs.md` §10.3.3 "Magnetorquers — physics of magnetic torque
  generation", §10.1.1 (magnetometer and coarse sun sensors), §10.1.2.
- **Outline:** τ = m × B: torque only perpendicular to the field. B-dot, m = −k·dB/dt,
  needs no attitude knowledge, which is why it is the first mode after separation.
  Rates decay roughly exponentially ("halve every 10–15 min"). Pointing with rods alone
  is slow and coarse because the field must rotate over the orbit, which is why
  COARSE_SUN on magnetorquers hunts and why the wheels (COM-004) matter. It closes with
  the operator checks: field magnitude plausible (5 000–70 000 nT), magnetometer and
  magnetorquer enable flags, and "a zero field means a blind law".
- **Route:** Manim (field vector, body frame, torque plane), then an `attitude3d.js`
  ui-viz replay of the detumble segment.
- **Prepares:** WB-5.5-L007 q4 and q5; WB-5.5-L005 q1 and q4; the "Detumble Failure"
  anomaly in OPERATIONS_GUIDE.
- **Audience:** `aocs` (essential); `flight_director` (recommended).
- **Timing:** best released after the AOCS COARSE_SUN fix, so the hold footage agrees.

### 3. AOCS modes and who moves them: commanded vs automatic transitions (3–4 min)

- **Textbook:** `18_operations_and_fdir.md` §18.2 "Operational modes", §18.2.2
  "Mode-transition state machine", §18.1.2 "Autonomy: where the line falls";
  `sim/docs/plan_2026-08c/AOCS_MODE_TRANSITIONS.md`.
- **Outline:** The EOSAT-1 AOCS mode ladder, OFF → DETUMBLE → COARSE_SUN → (star
  tracker) → finer modes. Which edges the ground commands (OFF → DETUMBLE in LEOP-007)
  and which the spacecraft takes on its own (DETUMBLE → COARSE_SUN on 0.5 °/s for 30 s).
  Commanding an automatic edge is wrong: you "verify" something you forced, and a
  COARSE_SUN entered above threshold will not hold. COARSE_SUN is where LEOP parks,
  because promotion needs a star tracker. Also covered: why a mode number is never a GO
  by itself, since the physics (field, rates, error) must agree.
- **Route:** Motion Canvas state-machine diagram with the edges firing, intercut with
  the console mode row.
- **Prepares:** WB-5.5-L007 q5 ("mode 2 so GO?"); WB-5.5-L005 q3 (do not command mode
  3); the LEOP-005 Step 6 parking-state note; and later the COM-003/COM-004 cards.
- **Audience:** whole team (the principle); `aocs` for the thresholds.

### 4. (smaller) Housekeeping on demand vs periodic: SIDs, HK_ENABLE and GET_PARAM (3 min)

- **Sources:** `sim/docs/PUS_SERVICE_REFERENCE.md` (S3, S20); `sim/docs/PARAMETER_MAP.md`.
- **Outline:** A powered unit is not a reporting unit. Covers periodic SIDs vs one-shot
  requests, parameters with no SID (0x0146/0x0147, 0x0215, 0x0260), and hex vs decimal
  parameter IDs in the command form.
- **Route:** Motion Canvas packet stream.
- **Prepares:** LEOP-004 Steps 2–3; LEOP-007 Step 3; LEOP-005 Step 4.
- **Audience:** whole team; `fdir_systems` in depth.
