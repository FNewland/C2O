## Knock-on effects checked

- **Power budget, planner, link budget.** The EPS model is energy-based, so generation, load, SoC and DoD do not change. Only the voltage telemetry moves. `tools/power_budget.py`, the planner's SoC budget and the link budget do not read battery voltage.
- **Behaviour.** Every protection fires at the same state of charge as before. The exception is the UV flag and the 0x0103 warning event, which now fire at the shed level (≈ 46 %) instead of inside the nominal band (≈ 65–71 %). The ground yellow and red limits on 0x0105 were inside the nominal band too, and now sit on the named levels.
- **Thermal.** Heater wattages are unchanged in config, so the code fallbacks only matter in reduced harnesses.
- **GSD.** Unchanged (33.4 m / 273 km), so imaging footprints are not affected.
- **Docs updated** (EN and French twins): manual chapters 00, 01, 08, 09, 10 and the walkthrough; 34 procedure cards with their FR twins; the course workbook EN/FR (`gen_worksheets.py` re-run); `OPERATIONS_GUIDE`, `PUS_SERVICE_REFERENCE` (with FR) and `SIMULATION_CONFIG`; regenerated `PARAMETER_MAP`, XTCE and SMDL.

## Left for the design side or the owner (not changed here)

- The course textbook `design/docs/course/orbit_north_week3.md`, its FR twin, `instructor_book.md` and `facilitator_book_expanded.md` still quote 21.5 / 26.4 / 29.2 V. The facilitator book also calls the imager "TriScape100 class". These files are on the design side.
- `design/configs/validation/eosat_6u_reference.yaml` and `configs/templates/6u_eo_cubesat.yaml` still say 450 km and LTAN 10:30. `design/.../mass_properties.py` still uses z = nadir.
- Owner question: the BPX has an **integrated 6 W heater**, while EOSAT-1 budgets an 8 W battery heater circuit (tcs.yaml, manual).
- Owner question: the S-band transceiver's datasheet input is 11–28 V, below the battery bus (24–33.4 V). A PCDU conversion stage is implied but not modelled.
- Owner question: the array MPP voltage (`_SA_MPP_V` 32.4 V) is below the BPX charge voltage (up to 33.6 V), which needs a buck-boost MPPT. This is not modelled.
- Not changed: the readout rates (244 Hz / 96 Mbps) are still sized at the retired 450 km. At 513 km the velocity-matched line rate is about 211 Hz.
