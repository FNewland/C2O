# SpaceCDF — A Course Textbook

## What this volume covers

The SpaceCDF Course Textbook is the companion volume to the
University of Ottawa's Concurrent Design Facility programme. It is
organised in two complementary parts.

**Part 1 — CubeSat Design** walks the lifecycle of a small spacecraft
from mission need through to integration test. Across twenty
chapters it covers the systems-engineering framework (the V-model,
the 17 NASA SEH technical processes, the ECSS lifecycle), the
Canadian space ecosystem and regulatory environment, international
standards for space systems, requirements engineering and trade
studies, orbit and mission architecture, the eight major
subsystems (EPS, AOCS, TCS, OBDH, TT&C, payload, propulsion,
structures), and the V&V / risk / cost framework that closes a
preliminary design review.

**Part 2 — CubeSat Operations** turns the design over to a control
room. It documents the **nine operator positions**, the **mission
control consoles** they sit at, the **voice-loop discipline** that
binds the team, the **nominal procedures** flown pass-by-pass, the
**LEOP and commissioning sequences**, the **operator calculations**
every shift requires, and the **anomaly response cue cards** that
turn a fault into a recoverable contingency. Procedures are
presented in aircraft-QRH challenge-response format with
operator-write-in space for actual telemetry values.

The volume is published with two companion books:

- **The Course Workbook** — exercises, worksheets, and operational
  drills that the student completes alongside each chapter.
- **The Instructor Book** — simulator setup, anomaly scenarios,
  breakpoints, and instructor protocols (not for trainees on
  console).

Three appendices close the volume: an **engineering-position
reference** for the design phase, **position quick-reference
cards** for the operations phase, and a **verification & first-
principles reference** that re-derives every formula in the body
of the textbook from first principles, with cross-method sanity
checks suitable for the worked verification trail at PDR.

## About the author

Dr Franz Newland is a Senior Lecturer in the Faculty of
Engineering at the University of Ottawa, leading the School of
Engineering Design and Teaching Innovation (SEDTI) and the
SpaceCDF programme.

**Claude**, an AI authoring assistant from Anthropic, is
credited as co-author for content drafting, technical
consolidation, figure generation, and structural editing
across the volume. All factual content and technical claims
were reviewed and approved by Dr Newland. The textbook draws on decades of published
systems-engineering, mission-operations, and astrodynamics
literature, refined across multiple cohorts of the SpaceCDF and
OrbitNorth programmes.
