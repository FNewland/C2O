# Lunar Gateway and Canadarm3: status as of 2026-10-28

Errata entry E1-3.08 (OER ch. 2 vs facilitator book). Researched 2026-10-28 by WebSearch/WebFetch. The SpaceNews article could not be fetched (robots); everything below rests on the other sources listed.

## Findings

1. **Gateway was paused on 24 March 2026, and the press reported it as a cancellation.**
   * NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman announced at NASA's "Ignition" event that NASA "intends to pause Gateway in its current form" and will shift to a phased lunar surface base, reusing suitable hardware and partner commitments. The base would cost about USD 20 billion through 2033 (NASA news release, 24 March 2026, updated 2 April 2026).
   * NASA's own word is "pause". The press called it "effectively cancelled" or "cancelled" (The Register, Novaspace, IFLScience, Reuters/AFP syndications).
   * Wikipedia's infobox lists the status as "Paused". It reports that the PPE is being repurposed for the SR-1 Freedom nuclear-electric mission and that a NASA OIG report on post-termination management is dated 24 June 2026.
2. **Congress.** Gateway was funded by the July 2025 reconciliation act (USD 2.6 billion). Reports say references to Gateway were dropped from 2026 funding legislation. I found no congressional action after March 2026 that restored Gateway (low-confidence negative finding).
3. **ESA.**
   * ESA was consulting its member states after March 2026, with a plan due at the June 2026 Council.
   * I-Hab continues through CDR before any decision on repurposing it. Lunar Link is being assessed for reuse as a lunar communications satellite, and Lunar View is slowed (European Spaceflight; Wikipedia).
   * The 347th Council (16–17 June 2026) deferred the exploration roadmap to the December 2026 Ministerial in Italy.
4. **CSA and Canadarm3.**
   * CSA President Lisa Campbell said NASA had briefed her before the announcement (The Logic, 10 April 2026). The CSA business plan of 13 March 2026 still listed Gateway.
   * MDA said in March 2026 that the Canadarm3 contract (with the CSA, about CAD 1 billion) was continuing.
   * In early August 2026 (MDA's response is dated 6 August 2026), **the CSA announced it will continue the Canadarm3 programme and repurpose Canadarm3 technologies for lunar-surface operations under Artemis**, with possible commercial low-Earth-orbit uses. The contract scope is unchanged; the use of the designs is being adjusted (SpaceQ, 7 August 2026; Payload, 10 August 2026; MDA Space, 6 August 2026).
   * NASA's base plan also names Canada's Lunar Utility Vehicle as a phase-3 contribution.
5. **Astronaut seat.** Jeremy Hansen flew on Artemis II (2026), and the Canadarm3 commitment had secured that seat. I found no published statement on future Canadian seats tied to the restructured commitment.

## Verdict

The evidence is clear on the March 2026 pause and cancellation. The facilitator book's "cancelled in March 2026" is defensible, but **"Canadarm3's role is under review" is out of date**: since August 2026 the programme is confirmed and being repurposed for lunar-surface operations. The OER's "Canadarm3 for the Lunar Gateway / In development" and "Lunar Gateway: astronaut flights planned" are out of date. The OER is updated, with dated wording, in ch. 2 EN and FR (milestones table, focus-area table, partnerships table, Problem 6, and new references 12–13).
