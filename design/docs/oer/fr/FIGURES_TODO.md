# French edition — figures still carrying English labels

Follow-up for the French PDFs (packet B1, 2026-10-28). Inline `<svg>` diagrams in the chapters are already
translated and are not listed.

**OER figures — DONE (packet FG1, 2026-10-28).** All 50 generators are language-aware through
`docs/assets/figures/i18n_fig.py` (hooked from `oer_style.apply_style()`/`save()`; no per-string edits in the
generators). `FIG_LANG=fr` writes `fig_<slug>_fr.png` beside the English, with the text from
`docs/assets/figures/i18n_fr_strings.py` (European French per `i18n/STYLE_FR.md` and `glossary.csv`: decimal comma
in labels, ticks and mathtext, no-break space before units, `:` `;` and inside « », k€, Go/Mo, PIRE, TEB, SCAO;
flight-software mode names, WBS numbers and item IDs unchanged). `python3 make_all.py` now builds English then
French (`--lang en|fr|all`) and fails on any untranslated string (`FR-MISSING`). English output is byte-identical
to the pre-change PNGs (SHA-256, 50/50). `build_oer.py --lang fr` swaps every `fig_*.png|svg` for its `_fr` twin
when present (falls back to English otherwise) and reports the count: 50 French, 0 fallback. No `.svg` figure
files are referenced by the OER chapters. Each French PNG was checked visually for overflow and clipping. Overlaps
that were already in the English versions are inherited, e.g. the GEO label clipped at the right edge of
`fig_fspl`, and labels over curves in `fig_beta_eclipse` and `fig_cost_scurve`.
